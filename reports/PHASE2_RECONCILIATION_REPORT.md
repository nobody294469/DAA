## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 2 of the clinical document-to-EHR conversion pipeline encompasses the **Hybrid Extraction Router and Subsystem Extraction Baselines** (Experiments E1–E4).

### Architectural Insights from Standalone E2 & E3 Evaluations:
1. **GLiNER-BioMed (E2):** Confirms high standalone utility on unstructured and semi-structured medication entities (`med.name` F1: 0.9448, `med.dose` F1: 0.9424), but failure on tabular lab grids without spatial awareness (`lab.value` F1: 0.2741; spurious predictions: 922 vs 705 GT). This provides empirical validation for DEC-001/DEC-004 routing policy: deterministic parsers handle structured grids, while GLiNER acts strictly as a fallback.
2. **Schema-Constrained LLM (E3):** Offline batch runner confirms strict adherence to `LLM_OUTPUT_SCHEMA` (DEC-013) and verbatim evidence-substring verification (§7.4). Zero schema parse errors or span hallucination leaks occurred across all 20 narrative documents.
3. **Canonical Field Vocabulary (DEC-017 / DEC-019):** Both E2 and E3 confirm the strict architectural scoping of Tier 1 vs. Tier 2 fields: Tier 2 fields (`lab.test_name`, `lab.unit`, `lab.reference_range`, `med.duration`) are strictly excluded from learned extractors, yielding an intended empirical F1 of 0.0 on those fields for E2 and E3.
