## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Methodology

This matrix provides an exhaustive, section-by-section requirements completeness audit of the clinical document-to-EHR conversion and verification system strictly against `PROJECT_SPEC.md` v1.0.

In accordance with strict audit directives:
1. No assumption of completion is made based on high-level phase sign-offs.
2. Every explicit RFC-2119 requirement (`MUST`, `MUST NOT`, `SHOULD`, `SHOULD NOT`, `MAY`), acceptance criterion, architectural contract, verification gate, and required experiment is individually audited.
3. Every item is assigned one of the seven exact authorized classifications:
   - `COMPLETE`: Evidence exists in repository and requirement is fully satisfied.
   - `COMPLETE WITH DOCUMENTED LIMITATION`: Requirement is satisfied to the extent permitted by the spec, with an explicit documented limitation.
   - `PARTIALLY COMPLETE`: Some required implementation/evidence exists, but a required portion is missing.
   - `NOT COMPLETE`: Required work has not been executed or produced.
   - `OPTIONAL`: Explicitly designated as optional in `PROJECT_SPEC.md`.
   - `FEASIBILITY-GATED`: Explicitly conditional or gated on external feasibility in `PROJECT_SPEC.md`.
   - `SUPERSEDED`: Historical requirement or artifact formally replaced by a later authoritative architectural decision recorded in `docs/decisions/`.
