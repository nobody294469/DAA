Five steps from page to bundle

Character offsets, page number and bounding box travel with every claim, from the PDF into the final FHIR resource.

  1. Ingest

    PyMuPDF reads born-digital PDFs into pages, lines and words, each with a bounding box. Scanned pages need OCR.

    PDF, PNG, JPG, TIFF

  2. Extract

    A layout router sends lab tables and medication grids to deterministic parsers and narrative notes to a schema-constrained LLM extractor.

    Evidence span: offsets + bbox

  3. Normalize

    Analytes map to LOINC, drugs to RxNorm and units to UCUM, with unit conversion before any comparison.

    LOINC, RxNorm, UCUM

  4. Verify

    Each claim is compared with its evidence and labelled Supported, Discrepancy or Insufficient evidence.

    • AStage A: DeterministicExact, numeric and flag rules
    • BStage B: ModernBERTFine-tuned, calibrated classifier
    • CStage C: LLM judgeQwen2.5-7B, escalations only

  5. FHIR and audit

    Verified claims become Observation, DiagnosticReport and Medication resources with claim-provenance extensions, plus a standalone audit report.

    FHIR R4B, DEC-016 provenance

Only the hard claims reach the LLM

A claim is settled at Stage B only when the deterministic and neural stages agree with enough confidence; everything else escalates to the Stage C judge.

What this is not

  1. Not a medical deviceA research prototype. It is not validated or approved for clinical decision support.
  2. Fidelity, not clinical truthA verdict says whether a claim matches its document, not whether the value, dose or drug is clinically appropriate.
  3. Synthetic benchmarkDocuments are rendered from Synthea patients with artificial degradation. No handwriting, faxes or real hospital records.
  4. Scans go through OCRBorn-digital PDFs are read directly. Scanned pages and images go through PaddleOCR, which is slower and can misread degraded text.
  5. Narrative extraction is the weak linkThe standalone narrative extractor was benchmarked with a mock LLM provider and has low recall.
  6. Stage C confidence is not calibratedThe LLM judge returns a fixed confidence, and escalated claims are harder: expect lower accuracy there than on claims settled at Stage B.
