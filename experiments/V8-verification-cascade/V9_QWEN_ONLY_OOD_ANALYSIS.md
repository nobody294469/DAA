## 5. Detailed Error Analysis (Direct Diagnostic Observations)

Inspection of all 116 misclassifications reveals specific, recurring failure mechanisms in unassisted Qwen-7B inference:

### 5.1 Error Distribution by Confusion Pair
1. **`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` $\rightarrow$ `DISCREPANCY`:** **45 errors (38.79% of all errors)**
   - *Direct Diagnostic Observation:* The claim specifies a clinical entity or value that is completely absent from the document (ground truth = `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`). Qwen frequently misinterprets this document-level absence as an active contradiction (`DISCREPANCY`), assigning contradiction types such as `value_mismatch` or `wrong_entity`.
   - *Example (`LAB_0029`):* Claim states `Lipase: 45.0 U/L`. Evidence span indicates analyte was not observed in the panel. Qwen rationale: *"The claim states 'Lipase: 45.0 U/L', but the source document indicates that the target analyte was not observed... this constitutes a value mismatch."*
2. **`SUPPORTED` $\rightarrow$ `DISCREPANCY`:** **33 errors (28.45% of all errors)**
   - *Direct Diagnostic Observation:* Occurs predominantly in `lab.flag` (15 errors), `lab.value` (11 errors), and `med.name` (4 errors).
   - In `lab.value`, minor formatting discrepancies (e.g., string representation `197 10*3/uL` vs OCR snippet `197.0 10*3/uL`) lead Qwen to reject perfectly supported claims.
   - In `lab.flag`, Qwen occasionally attempts numerical reference interval recalculation despite explicit flag tokens, contradicting the V1 prompt instruction in complex multiline headers.
3. **`DISCREPANCY` $\rightarrow$ `SUPPORTED`:** **15 errors (12.93% of all errors)**
   - *Direct Diagnostic Observation:* Subtle discrepancies (e.g., small dosage or concentration changes) were overlooked by Qwen and misjudged as supported.
4. **`DISCREPANCY` $\rightarrow$ `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`:** **9 errors (7.76% of all errors)**
5. **`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` $\rightarrow$ `SUPPORTED`:** **9 errors (7.76% of all errors)**
6. **`SUPPORTED` $\rightarrow$ `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`:** **5 errors (4.31% of all errors)**

## 7. Granular Metric Comparisons

### 7.1 Accuracy & Error Burden
- Standalone Qwen (V9) committed **116 errors**, which is:
  - **34 more errors** than ModernBERT standalone (82 errors).
  - **70 more errors** than the V1 selective cascade (46 errors).
- The selective cascade achieves a **60.3% relative reduction in total errors** compared with standalone Qwen (46 vs 116 errors).

### 7.2 Macro-F1 Discrepancy
- Standalone Qwen Macro-F1 dropped to **0.8543**, primarily driven by low recall on `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` (recall = 0.5462, F1 = 0.6566).
- In contrast, the V1 cascade achieved **0.9538 Macro-F1**, preserving balanced tripartite classification across all three clinical labels.

### 7.3 Domain-Specific Complementarity (`lab.flag` vs `lab.value`)
- **Where Qwen Excels:** In `lab.flag`, Qwen achieves **0.9085 accuracy** (29 errors), substantially outperforming ModernBERT standalone (0.8170 accuracy, 58 errors).
- **Where ModernBERT Excels:** In `lab.value`, ModernBERT achieves **0.9844 accuracy** (only 17 errors), dramatically outperforming standalone Qwen (0.9396 accuracy, 66 errors).
- **Synergy in Cascade:** The selective cascade leverages ModernBERT for the high-volume, exact-matching `lab.value` claims while selectively routing ambiguous `lab.flag` cases to Qwen.

## 8. Architectural Implications for the Selective Cascade

The empirical findings from Experiment V9 illuminate the core system dynamics:

1. **Standalone LLM Inference is Sub-Optimal in both Accuracy and Efficiency:**
   - Deploying Qwen-7B as an unconstrained, standalone verifier not only increases compute time by **3.9$\times$** compared to the cascade (103.4 min vs 26.6 min), but also results in **2.5$\times$ more errors** (116 vs 46 errors).
2. **Complementary Error Topographies:**
   - Neural cross-encoders (ModernBERT) and autoregressive LLMs (Qwen) exhibit distinct, non-overlapping failure modes:
     - ModernBERT struggles with complex spatial flag indicators in tabular layouts (`lab.flag`).
     - Qwen struggles with strict absence discrimination (`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` vs `DISCREPANCY`) and exact numeric string grounding (`lab.value`).
3. **The Selective Cascade Earns Its Complexity:**
   - Rather than functioning as a redundant layer, the selective cascade routes claims so that each architecture operates within its regime of strength.
   - The cascade achieves superior accuracy (97.48%) to either model running alone (ModernBERT: 95.51%, Qwen: 93.65%).
